Thermodynamics: Problem Generator

Practice problems for an undergraduate engineering thermodynamics course, generated on demand. Each problem is a fresh variant of a classic textbook exercise, with its own numbers, a figure, an answer checker, and a worked solution. The chapters follow the order of the standard text.

How to use

Choose a chapter, pick a problem type, and press New problem. Enter your answers and press Check answers; numerical answers within 2% are accepted. Show the solution when you are done. Every problem has a seed number in the page address, so a link to it brings up the same problem for anyone you share it with. Property data for water, refrigerant R-134a, and common gases are computed in the page, so no tables are required.
