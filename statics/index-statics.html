Statics: Problem Generator

Problems in the style of a standard undergraduate statics course, generated on demand. Each has its own numbers, geometry and figure, an answer checker, and a worked solution.

How to use

Choose a chapter, pick a problem type, and press New problem. Enter your answers and press Check answers; numerical answers within 2% are accepted. Show the solution when you are done. Every problem has a seed number in the page address, so a link to it brings up the same problem for anyone you share it with. Chapters marked in preparation will be added as they are completed.
