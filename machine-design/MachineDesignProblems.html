Machine Design: Problem Generator

Practice problems for an undergraduate machine design course, generated on demand. Each problem is a fresh variant of a classic textbook exercise, with its own numbers, geometry and figure, an answer checker, and a worked solution.

How to use

Choose a chapter, pick a problem type, and press New problem. Enter your answers and press Check answers; numerical answers within 2% are accepted. Show the solution when you are done. The synthesis page works differently: enter a design and press Evaluate design to see whether it meets every requirement. Every problem has a seed number in the page address, so a link to it brings up the same problem for anyone you share it with.
